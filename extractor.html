Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the extractor tool.

Is this legal? Can I extract assets from any extension?
Yes, extracting publicly available assets (icons, screenshots) from the Chrome Web Store for research and educational purposes is generally permissible under fair use. However, we recommend respecting copyright and not redistributing the assets without permission. Use this tool responsibly.
How does the extraction work?
The tool uses a public proxy service to fetch the extension's page and parse the manifest and asset URLs. All extraction happens server‑side through our secure proxy — your IP is never exposed.
What formats are the extracted assets in?
Icons are exported as PNG files with their original sizes. Screenshots are downloaded as JPG/PNG as provided by the store. Everything is bundled into a convenient ZIP archive.
Why is there a daily limit?
The free tier is limited to 3 extractions per day to prevent abuse and keep the proxy service sustainable. Upgrade to Premium Pro for unlimited extractions and priority access.
Can I extract assets from private or unlisted extensions?
No, the extractor only works with public Chrome Web Store listings. Private extensions are not accessible via the public store API.
