Frequently asked questions

Quick answers to the most common questions.

What file formats do you support?
We accept PNG, JPG, WebP, and Figma exports. All output is delivered as PNG (with transparency) or JPEG (flattened to 24‑bit RGB).
Is my asset stored on your servers?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images are never uploaded to any server — privacy by design.
What happens when I hit the free limit?
The free tier allows 3 conversions per day. After that, you'll be prompted to upgrade to Premium Pro for unlimited access.
Can I use the icons in commercial projects?
Yes. Premium Pro and Enterprise licenses grant full commercial rights. You can deploy them in SaaS products, browser extensions, and client work.
Do you offer refunds?
Yes. We offer a 14‑day money‑back guarantee on all paid plans. No questions asked.
