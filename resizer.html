Frequently asked questions

Got questions about the resizer tool? We've got answers.

What image formats can I upload?
We accept PNG, JPEG, WebP, and most common image formats. If you're exporting from Figma, use PNG or JPG.
Are my images uploaded to a server?
No. All processing happens entirely in your browser. Your images never leave your device — privacy by design.
What does the ZIP contain?
The ZIP includes: `icons/` with 16, 32, 48, 128px PNGs; `store-visuals/` with 1280×800, 440×280, and 1400×560 promo tiles — all flattened to 24‑bit RGB to pass Chrome Store validation.
Why would I use the JPEG pipeline?
The Chrome Web Store rejects promo images with alpha (transparency). The JPEG pipeline flattens your image over a white background, producing a 24‑bit compliant file that passes validation every time.
